Rules
The Basics

Flick the ball to hit the green target zone on the crossbar. Hit it twice to progress to the next level.

Hazards
  • Wind: Pushes your ball off-course mid-flight.
  • Crowd: Shakes the screen and ball under pressure.
  • Glare: Blinds your view of the target.
Bonus Rounds

Keep your eyes on the sky! Every 10 wins (starting after Level 14), a special Winged Money Bag will appear.

During this bonus round, all hazards are disabled and you must rely entirely on your ⚡ Basic Kick. The bag will teleport and hover in the sky for 2 seconds at a time. Strike the bag to earn a massive 500 Token bonus!

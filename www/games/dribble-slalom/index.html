Rules
How to Play
  • Win Condition: Win best 2 of 3 attempts to level up.
  • Pass through the glowing circles.
  • Do not hit the cones, or you recoil back 1 space.
  • The Red Ghost will hunt you. Do not let it catch your ball!
Bonuses & Hazards
  • Conditions will show up in later levels: sun glare, rain and crowd noise.
    • Sun Glare: Bright flashes that temporarily blind your view.
    • Rain: Slippery ground that makes the ball slide further.
    • Crowd Noise: Distracting cheers that make the ball jitter and shake.     Purchase special gear to reduce the effect of these conditions.
  • Golden Gate: A rare Yellow Gate appears occasionally. Trace through it to instantly earn a massive 1000 Token bonus!
  • Pass through blue Special Gates to charge your Special Moves.

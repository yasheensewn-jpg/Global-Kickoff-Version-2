Rules
How to Play
  • Shoot: Swipe up from the red ball toward an opponent to hit them.
  • Win: Knock out enough opponents to reach the point target before time runs out.
  • Lose: If opponents cross the red dotted line, you get a Strike. Too many strikes = Game Over!
Bonuses & Hazards
  • Tap or shoot flying clocks for +5 Seconds.
  • Tap or shoot flying bags for +20 Tokens.
  • At higher levels, watch out for the Headache Blur! Tap the Sports Drink to cure it temporarily.
  • Collect special moves to use, aim the ball at them when they appear in the sky. Need to be purchased first.
